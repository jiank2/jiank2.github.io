Research

My research aims to build trustworthy AI agents and systems that respect individual preferences and values.

Trustworthy AI

Are model outputs trustworthy? How can we ensure the reliability of model and agentic behaviors?

Adaptive Personalization

How can we adapt agentic behavior to individual values and preferences in a consistent and reliable way?

If your research interests include trustworthy AI, generative AI (e.g., LLMs, diffusion models, flow models), graph mining, personalization, and their applications in health and scientific domains, please feel free to reach out!
