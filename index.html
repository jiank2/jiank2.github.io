Research

My research aims to model and understand the interconnected world (primarily with graphs). It mainly investigates the following questions in three big themes:
  • Data: Where do the data come from? What is the optimal data for a learning task?
  • Model: Can we trust the model outputs?
  • Task: How to let a learning model solve complicated real-world tasks?
